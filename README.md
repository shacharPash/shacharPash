# Hi, I'm Shahar 👋

I love building products: taking an idea all the way to something people actually use.
Here are a few things I built. The tools are free and open source, so feel free to use them.

## 🚀 Products

- **[What's Playing Today?](https://hebrew-movie-search.vercel.app/?lang=en)**<br>Find something to watch in Israel, in Hebrew or English. Keep favorites without an account, and pick something together in a shared room.
- **[On the Way](https://on-the-way-nine.vercel.app)**<br>A driver and a passenger compare pickup spots along the route, within the detour the driver is willing to make.
- **[Zmunah](https://shabat-posts.vercel.app)**<br>Personal Shabbat and Omer posters in Hebrew, with candle-lighting times for Israel.

## 🛠️ Tools

- **[speak-to-paste](https://github.com/shacharPash/speak-to-paste)**<br>Press a shortcut, speak, press it again: your words are pasted wherever your cursor is, in any Mac app. Hebrew-first, fully local, one-command install.
- **[CallRec](https://github.com/shacharPash/callrec)**<br>Records your calls on your Mac and transcribes them, in Hebrew or English. Starts by itself when a Zoom, Meet, Slack or Teams call starts. Fully local, one-command install.

## 🤖 How I work

I build and run my own team of AI agents. Each one has a job: drafting my messages, handling PR review comments, watching CI, keeping my work log. They do the legwork, I make the calls, and nothing goes out without my approval. Most of my tools started as fixes for my own workday.
