# Hi, I'm Shahar

I love building products: taking an idea all the way to something people actually use.
Here are a few things I built. The tools are free and open source, so feel free to use them.

## Products

- **[מה רואים היום?](https://hebrew-movie-search.vercel.app)** Find something to watch in Israel. Search movies and shows in Hebrew or English, keep favorites without an account, and pick something together in a shared room.
- **[On the Way](https://on-the-way-nine.vercel.app)** A driver and a passenger compare pickup spots along the driving route, within the detour the driver is willing to make.
- **[Zmunah](https://shabat-posts.vercel.app)** Personal Shabbat and Omer posters in Hebrew, with candle-lighting times for Israel.

## Tools

- **[speak-to-paste](https://github.com/shacharPash/speak-to-paste)** Press a shortcut, speak, press it again: your words are pasted wherever your cursor is, in any Mac app. Hebrew-first, fully local, one-command install.
- **[CallRec](https://github.com/shacharPash/callrec)** Records your calls on your Mac and gives you a transcript, in Hebrew or English. It can start by itself when a call starts in Zoom, Meet, Slack or Teams. Fully local, one-command install.
- **[pr-review-assistant](https://github.com/shacharPash/pr-review-assistant)** A local GitHub PR review app: a real diff view, commit-range comparison, drafted inline comments and optional AI explanations, submitted from your own account.

## Open source

**[NRedisStack](https://github.com/redis/NRedisStack)**, the official .NET client for Redis. I made its first commit in 2022 and I'm its top contributor, with 100 merged PRs. It has more than 13 million downloads on NuGet.

I also contributed to [redis-py](https://github.com/redis/redis-py), [node-redis](https://github.com/redis/node-redis), [jedis](https://github.com/redis/jedis), [StackExchange.Redis](https://github.com/seredis/StackExchange.Redis), [predis](https://github.com/predis/predis), [redis-om-dotnet](https://github.com/redis/redis-om-dotnet), [RediSearch](https://github.com/RediSearch/RediSearch) and [RedisBloom](https://github.com/RedisBloom/RedisBloom).
